> # What is a Large Language Model? 
A large language model (LLM) is an advanced type of artificial intelligence designed to read, summarize, translate, and generate human-like text.
## How LLMs Work

* Prediction: An LLM works by guessing the next most likely word or piece of a word in a sequence based on the text it receives.
* Training Data: These models train on massive amounts of data, including books, websites, and articles, to learn grammar, facts, and human communication styles.
* Transformers: Most modern LLMs use a neural network design called a transformer, which helps the system understand the context and relationships between words in a sentence.

## Common Uses

* Chatbots: Powering conversational assistants like OpenAI's ChatGPT or Google Gemini.
* Content Creation: Drafting essays, writing code, or summarizing long reports.
* Language Translation: Converting text accurately from one language to another. [1, 4]
